# My Favorite Movie: The Book of Life

## About the Movie
**The Book of Life** is a *animated musical fantasy* film released in 2014. It was directed by Jorge R. Gutiérrez and produced by Guillermo del Toro. The story is set around the Mexican holiday **Día de los Muertos** (Day of the Dead).

> A story about love, family, and remembering the people who came before us.

---

## The Story
Manolo is a young man who comes from a family of bullfighters but would rather follow his heart and play music. He and his friends María and Joaquín grow up together in the town of San Ángel. Two gods, La Muerte and Xibalba, make a bet on which of the two boys will marry María. This sets off an adventure that takes Manolo through the Land of the Remembered and the Land of the Forgotten.

## Main Characters
- **Manolo Sánchez** - a musician who is torn between family tradition and his dream
- **María Posada** - a strong, smart, and independent young woman
- **Joaquín Mondragón** - a brave soldier and Manolo's best friend
- **La Muerte** - the kind ruler of the Land of the Remembered
- **Xibalba** - the tricky ruler of the Land of the Forgotten

## Why I Like It
1. The **story** is about love, courage, and being true to yourself
2. The **colorful art style** looks like wooden figures and Mexican folk art
3. The **music** has mariachi-style versions of popular songs
4. It shows how important it is to **remember our family**
